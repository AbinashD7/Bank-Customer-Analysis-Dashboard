# 🏦 Bank Customer Analysis Dashboard
This project presents an interactive **Bank Customer Analysis Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to analyze customer demographics, loyalty tiers, loan distribution, and risk levels. The dashboard provides analysts and banking teams with key insights into customer composition, financial exposure, and growth trends across nationalities and age groups.

---

## 🔍 Project Overview
This dashboard provides comprehensive insights into:
- Total Customers, Card Balance, Deposits, Loans & Average Income
- Customer Distribution by Nationality and Sex
- Customer Loyalty Tier Breakdown
- Loan Customers & Card Usage by Age Group
- Customer Risk Level Segmentation
- Client Growth Trends Over Time
- Top Occupations by Total Bank Loans

## 📈 Key Insights & Features
- KPI Cards displaying:
  - Total Customers (2.94K)
  - Total Card Balance (9.33M)
  - Total Deposits (1.97bn)
  - Total Loans (1.74bn)
  - Average Income (171.47K)
- Interactive slicers for:
  - Nationality (African, American, Asian, Australian, European)
  - "All" dropdown filter
- Donut chart breakdown of customers by nationality (European is the largest group)
- Donut chart of customers by loyalty tier (Jade 44.35%, Silver 25.65%, Gold 19.52%, Platinum 10.48%)
- Loan customers by age group (Young 756, Adult 739, Middle-aged 732, Senior 687)
- Number of cards by age group (Young ~26%, Middle-aged 25.48%, Adult 25.04%, Senior 23.23%)
- Risk level distribution from 1 to 5 (Level 2 largest with 1,200 customers, Level 5 smallest with 156)
- Client growth trend from 1995 to 2020, with a sharp spike near 2020 (peak of 244 clients)
- Customer breakdown by nationality and sex (European: 672 female, 601 male)
- Top 5 occupations by total bank loans (Accountant 47M, Database Administrator 46M, Web Developer 44M, Office Assistant 43M, Human Resources Assistant 43M)

## 🚀 Outcome
This dashboard enables users to:
- Understand the overall size and financial position of the customer base
- Identify which nationalities and age groups make up the largest customer segments
- Assess loyalty tier distribution to support retention and rewards strategies
- Monitor customer risk levels to support credit and lending decisions
- Pinpoint occupations with the highest loan exposure
- Track customer acquisition trends and identify periods of rapid growth
- Support data-driven marketing and risk management strategies through interactive visualizations
